What is SST Connect?

SST Connect is an internal Student Support Team (SST) management platform designed to organize and streamline school support operations. It helps centralize support requests, team discussions, caseload assignments, and student documentation.

Important System Purpose Note:

SST Connect is NOT intended to replace existing school systems or databases. It serves as an internal operational tool to support SST processes, improve collaboration, and centralize student support information.

📋

Referral Management

Processes incoming Google Form referrals, allows intake reviews, tier designations, and support personnel assignments.

📂

Student Support Documentation

Centralizes Approaches to Learning (ATL), Anecdotal Records, Parent Meetings, and structured Support Plans in one place.

👥

Caseload & Workspace Management

Provides tailored personal dashboards based on user email logins, categorizing active caseloads by urgency status.

Referral & Case Onboarding Workflow

Standard student support referrals follow a structured multi-step progression from initial submission to active case management. Follow the interactive diagram below to explore each stage of the intake process.

Standard Referral Progression

1 Google Form Referral Submitted by staff
➔
2 Referral Responses SST Continuum Only
➔
3 Review & Discuss Continuum with SST Team
➔
4 Assign SST / EAL Continuum sets Tier & Team
5 Case Appears in My Workspace Linked automatically via user's login email
➔
6 & 7 Input Profile & Ongoing Support Add to Student Profile; record Anecdotal/Meetings/Plans

Direct Entry Shortcut for Existing Cases

For students who are already part of an SST member's caseload, you do not need to submit or process a referral form. The student can be added directly using the creation form inside Student Profile.

Main Features & Application Modules

SST Connect provides specialized modules tailored for monitoring student counts, tracking urgency tiers, reviewing consultation notes, and maintaining comprehensive case documentation across academic years.

Home / Homepage

All Roles

The landing page when opening SST Connect. Displays total active student counts for the academic year selected on the homepage, filtered by urgency status:

  • Total Active Students
  • Total WS Students (Well-being & Safety)
  • Total PS Students (Priority of Support)
  • Total MS Students (Monitor of Support)
  • Quick Actions buttons

Overview Dashboard

All Roles (View Only for PVP)

Displays analytical charts detailing active student distribution by:

  • Urgency Status (MS, PS, WS) & Tiers
  • Presenting Issues & Gender
  • Students by Grade
  • Filtered views for WS, PS, and MS

Resources Center

Continuum & Members

Provides direct access to external tools and web links (shared documents, calendars, spreadsheets). Users can click listed links to open resources directly or add new links by filling in:

Name + Category + Link

Latest Anecdotal Records

Continuum & Members

Displays a unified, real-time feed of the latest anecdotal entries created across all active cases by all team users in one centralized timeline view.

Student Profile

Continuum & Members

Displays all active students in the SST caseload for the selected academic year, grouped by grade level. From here, staff can:

  • Add new students or edit existing details
  • Add or access ATL (Approaches to Learning)
  • Add or access Anecdotal Records
  • Add or access Parent Meetings & Support Plans

Consul Records / PYP Consult

Continuum & Members

Contains FYI student information used as discussion notes and reference material for meetings. The core database originates from Excel. Entries can be updated via Excel import or through the internal SST Connect form.

Categorized Views (MS/WS/PS)

Continuum & Members

Dedicated filtered pages showing active students for the selected academic year categorized by urgency status:

  • MS Students: Monitor of Support
  • WS Students: Well-being & Safety Support
  • PS Students: Priority of Support

Parent Meetings & Plans

Continuum & Members

Parent Meetings: Displays a complete list of parent meeting records created by users for their respective students.
Add Support Plan: A Home quick action that opens the Support Plan form directly, letting users select a student from a dropdown without opening Student Profile first.

Student Archive

Continuum & Members

Stores historical records for students who have been in the SST caseload across multiple academic years. Filterable by academic year and student status:

Active Inactive Graduated

SST Caseload Summary

Continuum & Members

Features a visual pie chart displaying the distribution of active students managed by each registered SST member in SST Connect.

Referral Responses

SST Continuum ONLY

Exclusive module for the SST Continuum to review incoming Google Form referral submissions, add notes, set tiers, and assign support personnel prior to official case setup.

My Workspace vs. Student Profile

My Workspace is a personalized operational dashboard connected directly to the email account used to log into SST Connect. It filters and presents only records relevant to your individual assigned workload.

Student Profile Module

School-Wide Scope
  • Shows all active students across the entire school for the selected academic year.
  • Organized and grouped strictly by grade level.
  • Allows adding new active students to the central caseload.
  • Accessible by all SST Continuum, SST Members, and EAL Teachers.

My Workspace Module

Personalized Email Scope
  • Filters exclusively to students assigned to your email login.
  • Divided into 4 individual workload streams.
  • Ensures quick access to active cases without searching through school-wide rosters.

The 4 Sections of My Workspace

1. My Students

Displays active students directly assigned to your email account as Assign SST or Assign EAL.

2. My Parent Meetings

Displays parent meeting records linked specifically to your assigned students or caseload.

3. My Support Plans

Displays active support plans created for or linked to your assigned caseload.

4. New Referrals

Displays newly referred cases assigned to you by the SST Continuum team.
